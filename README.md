# ABC-Executive-Summary-Profitability-Dashboard
An interactive Excel dashboard project focused on analyzing sales performance, revenue, cost of goods sold (COGS), gross profit, and profitability across different countries, regions, and sales channels using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and KPIs.
ABC Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard
📊 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive ABC Sales Executive Summary and Profitability Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel. It analyzes sales transactions to evaluate revenue generation, cost of goods sold (COGS), gross profit, gross profit margin, sales volume, and performance across time periods, geographic markets, and sales channels.
🎯 Project Objectives:
- Monitor overall revenue, COGS, gross profit, and gross profit margin.
- Track units sold and the number of sales transactions.
- Compare monthly revenue and gross profit performance.
- Evaluate revenue contribution by country and region.
- Compare sales performance across sales channels.
- Present key metrics and trends in a consolidated visual dashboard.

📁 Workbook Structure
The workbook contains three worksheets:
Worksheet	Purpose
SalesDataTable	Transaction-level source data formatted as an Excel Table
Pivot Table	Supporting summaries and KPI calculations used by the dashboard
Dashboard	Executive dashboard containing KPI summaries and visual charts
📊 Pivot Table Analysis:
The supporting Pivot Table worksheet includes the following analyses:
1. Monthly Revenue & Gross Profit – compares revenue and gross profit across all 12 months.
2. Country-wise Revenue – summarizes revenue by individual country.
3. Sales Channel-wise Revenue – compares revenue generated through Corporate, Marketplace, Online Store, and Retail Partners.
4. Region-wise Revenue – compares revenue across Central Europe, Northern Europe, Southern Europe, and Western Europe.
5. KPI Summary – consolidates key financial and sales-volume metrics for the dashboard.
📈 Dashboard Visualizations
The dashboard uses four chart types to communicate the analysis:
- Line Chart: Monthly trend comparison of revenue and gross profit.
- Bar Chart: Country-wise revenue comparison (the countries included in the supporting chart range).
- Pie Chart: Revenue contribution by sales channel.
- Doughnut Chart: Revenue contribution by region.

This dashboard can support business stakeholders by helping them:
- Monitor sales and profitability performance using a concise KPI summary.
- Identify stronger and weaker months for further investigation.
- Compare geographic markets and sales channels.
- Understand the relationship between revenue, COGS, and gross profit.
- Use summarized information to support sales planning and resource allocation.
